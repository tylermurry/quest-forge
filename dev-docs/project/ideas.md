# Ideas

Ideas for after 1.0.0. The 1.0.0 work (including the MCP server, game skins and close-up scenes) is
tracked in the [1.0.0 milestone](https://github.com/tylermurry/quest-forge/milestones).

- **Variables in rules.** Rules that hold and compare values, for example a pawn shop where you trade one item for another.
- **Richer dialogue.** Show a close-up of each character as they talk, with their mouth moving.
- **A demo game.** A small finished adventure made in the editor, offered as a "New project" template.
