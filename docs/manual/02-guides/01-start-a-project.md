# Start and save a project

Create a project for your game, find your way around the editor, and save your work.

## Create a new project

1. Open Quest Forge. You see the start screen.
2. Under **New project**, type your game's title.
3. Click **Create blank project**.
4. Choose the folder to put the project in. Quest Forge makes a new folder for your game inside it.

Your new game already has one scene, **First Scene**, with a stand-in background, a floor the player can walk
on, a spawn point and a player character. Click **Play** to walk around in it straight away.

## Open a project

- To open a game you've worked on recently, click it under **Recent projects**.
- To open any other project, click **Open folder…** and choose the project's folder.

## Find your way around

| Area | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Project Browser** (left) | Everything in your game. Double-click something to open it in a tab. |
| **Editor tabs** (middle) | The scene, item, character, dialogue or image you're working on. |
| **Inspector** (right) | The details and rules of what's selected. |
| **Problems** (bottom) | What's wrong with your game right now. |

To make room, hide panels you don't need:

- **View ▸ Project Browser** (⌘1 / Ctrl+1), **View ▸ Inspector** (⌘2 / Ctrl+2) and **View ▸ Problems**
  (⌘3 / Ctrl+3) show or hide each panel.
- **View ▸ Hide All Panels** (Ctrl+\\) hides everything except the editor tabs. Choose it again to bring
  them back.

## Save your work

Quest Forge doesn't save automatically. Click **Save** in the title bar, or press ⌘S / Ctrl+S. The title bar
says **Unsaved changes** until you do.

:::note Note
**Play** and **Build Game** use what's in the editor, including changes you haven't saved yet.
:::

## Undo and redo

Every change you make can be undone, one step at a time.

- **Undo**: ⌘Z / Ctrl+Z, or the left arrow in the title bar.
- **Redo**: ⌘⇧Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z (Ctrl+Y also works on Windows and Linux), or the right arrow.

The **Edit** menu says what will be undone, such as **Undo Delete hotspot**.

## Close a project

Click the back arrow at the left of the title bar, or choose **File ▸ Back to Project Selector**
(⌘⇧W / Ctrl+Shift+W). If you have unsaved changes, Quest Forge asks before closing.

## More

- If the project's files change on disk while it's open, for example from a backup tool, Quest Forge loads
  the changes.
- Undo remembers your last 500 changes.
