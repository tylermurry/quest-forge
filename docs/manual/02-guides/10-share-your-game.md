# Share your game

Turn your game into a single `.quest` file that anyone can play in a web browser.

## Build the game

1. Fix any errors in **Problems**. Building is blocked while there are errors.
2. Choose **File ▸ Build Game…** (⌘B / Ctrl+B).
3. Choose where to save the `.quest` file.

The file holds your whole game: every scene, rule, picture and sound.

## Share it

Send the `.quest` file however you like: email, a file-sharing link, a chat. It's one file, so there's nothing
to unpack.

## Play it

Players open the **Play** page on the Quest Forge website, click **Choose a .quest file**, and pick your file.
The game runs in the web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. They don't need to install anything.

## What players can do

| To | Players |
|---|---|
| Choose a verb | Move the pointer to the top of the game to show the icon bar, or tap the tab on a touch screen. Right-click to cycle through verbs. |
| See what something is | Hover over it, or touch and hold. |
| Skip text | Click or tap anywhere. |
| Use their items | Open **Inventory** from the icon bar. |
| Save, load or restart | Open **Settings** from the icon bar. There are ten save slots. |
| Change the volume | **Settings ▸ Volume**, for music and sound effects separately. |

## Update your game

Build again and share the new file. Players' saved games keep working with the new version.
