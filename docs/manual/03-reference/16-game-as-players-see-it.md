# The game as players see it

What your players see and how they control the game. It works the same in Playtest and in a web browser.

## Icon bar

Hidden until needed. With a mouse, move the pointer to the top of the game. On a touch screen, tap the small tab
at the top.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Walk** | Walk where you click. |
| **Look** | Look at what you click. |
| **Hand** | Touch, take or open what you click. |
| **Talk** | Talk to what you click. |
| Current item | Use the selected inventory item on what you click. |
| **Inventory** | Opens the inventory. |
| **Settings** | Opens the settings. |

The bar also shows the score: "Score: 5 of 25".

The game's maker can give the bar a frame and their own pictures for the buttons; they work the same way.

## Pointing and clicking

| Mouse | Touch | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Click | Tap | Acts on what's there with the current verb. |
| Right-click | — | Switches to the next verb: Walk, Look, Hand, Talk, then the current item. |
| Hover | Touch and hold | Shows the name of what's there. |

Clicking while text is showing skips it. Clicking somewhere new while the player is walking changes where they
go.

## Inventory

Shows the items the player carries, or "You aren't carrying anything." The buttons along it choose what clicking
an item does:

| Button | Clicking an item |
|---|---|
| Look | Shows its description. |
| Select | Makes it the current item and closes the inventory, ready to use on something in the scene. |
| Current item | Uses the current item on the clicked item, to combine them. |
| OK | Closes the inventory. |

## Dialogue choices

When a conversation offers choices, they appear in one box with the question above them. Click an answer to say it. Long lists have pages.

## Settings

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Restart** | Starts the game again. |
| **Save** | Saves to one of ten slots. Each slot shows where and the score, such as "Garden shed · Score 5 of 25". |
| **Load** | Loads a saved game. |
| **Volume** | Turns **Music** and **Sound effects** up or down separately. |
| **Text size** | Makes the game's text smaller or larger, from 75% to 200% of the size you chose, in steps of 25%. It starts at 100%. Narration, speech and choices grow fully; text on buttons and menus grows only as far as its button allows. |

Saves, volume and text size are kept in the player's web browser. Text size is the player's own choice; it never
changes your game. To change the size your game starts with, see [Game settings](./04-game-settings.md).

## Endings

| Ending | Shows | Buttons |
|---|---|---|
| Death | **You have died**, the message and the score. | **Restore**, **Restart**, **Try again** (back to when the player entered the scene). |
| Winning | **You won!** and the score. | **Restore**, **Restart**. |

## Screens of any size

The game scales to fit the window, keeping its shape, with black bars if needed. Every button is big enough to
tap on a phone, and nothing needs hover.
